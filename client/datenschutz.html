Privacy notice

1. Who is responsible

Fahim Ul Haque, Student, TU Darmstadt. Contact: see the imprint.

2. What TUDa Mail is

TUDa Mail is a web application that lets you use your Technical University of Darmstadt mailbox in your browser. When you sign in, the server connects to the university's mail servers (IMAP and SMTP) on your behalf. Your emails stay on the university's servers; TUDa Mail does not store message contents.

3. What data is processed

  • Sign-in data: your email address, TU-ID and password. The password is needed so the server can read your mailbox and send mail for you. It is held in the server's memory and, encrypted (AES-256-GCM), in a file on the server. It is deleted when you sign out, and at the latest 30 days after your last visit.
  • Sender name: the name you enter when you sign in.
  • Settings and content you create: signature, contacts, your notification subscription (see section 5), and markers that prevent a message from being sent twice (a check value, not the message).
  • Technical logs: time, page requested, result and IP address. Identifiers are stored in shortened (hashed) form. No message contents and no passwords. Purpose: security and troubleshooting. Logs are rotated automatically and kept only for a short time (a few days).
  • Cookie: one strictly necessary session cookie. No advertising, no analytics, no third-party services, no external fonts.

4. Legal basis

Providing the service: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Security and logs: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation). Notifications: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the menu).

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the server checks your mailbox for new mail about every 90 seconds and sends the sender and subject through your browser's push service (Google, Mozilla or Apple). The content is encrypted for your browser, so the push service cannot read it, but it learns that a notification is delivered. This may involve a transfer to the USA. Signing out or turning notifications off ends this.

6. Recipients and hosting

  • The mail servers of the Technical University of Darmstadt (where your emails are stored).
  • Hosting: Microsoft Azure, data centre in Sweden (EU), as a processor under Microsoft's standard data protection terms.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent. You delete most data yourself by signing out. For anything else, write to the contact given in the imprint. You can also complain to the supervisory authority: the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Wiesbaden (datenschutz.hessen.de).

8. Security

Encrypted connection (HTTPS), limits on sign-in attempts, encrypted storage of sessions and automatic security updates on the server. No system is completely secure: use a password you do not use anywhere else, and sign out on shared devices.
